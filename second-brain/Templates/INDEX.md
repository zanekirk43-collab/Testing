# Templates

Insert with the core Templates plugin (`{{date}}` and `{{title}}` are filled in on insert).

- [Daily note](Daily%20note.md): today's priorities, captures and reflection.
- [Weekly review](Weekly%20review.md): empty the inbox and update projects.
- [Project](Project.md): outcome, next actions and notes for a new project.
- [Note](Note.md): a general resource or idea note.
